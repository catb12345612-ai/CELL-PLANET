How Cell Planet Khammam ERP & POS Works

1. Instant Barcode & IMEI Scanning: Plug in any USB/Bluetooth barcode gun or click the quick scan chips. When a box barcode is scanned, the system automatically fetches the Model, Color, RAM/Storage Variant, Warranty, and Technical Specifications, and pops up the IMEI verification box so staff can scan the exact 15-digit IMEI in 1 second.
2. Flexible Admin GST Toggle (36ACVPK3564C1ZE): Want a GST Tax Invoice? Keep the "Apply GST (18%)" switch ON (supports both +18% Exclusive and 18% Inclusive MRP). Want a quick non-GST retail bill? Flip the switch OFF—the invoice automatically hides GSTIN and tax rows!
3. Complete Payment & Finance Tracking: Record Cash, Online/UPI (PhonePe/GPay/Paytm), Card (with last 4 digits), Finance (Bajaj Finserv, IDFC First, HDB, TVS Credit with Down Payment, Loan Amount & Approval ID), or Split Payments, plus Old Phone Trade-In deductions.
4. 80mm Thermal + A4 Print + Direct WhatsApp Invoice: After billing, choose between 80mm Thermal Receipt or A4 Full Executive Invoice, and click Send on WhatsApp to send the complete itemized receipt with IMEI directly to the customer's WhatsApp.
5. End-of-Day Excel Download & Firebase Console Clear: Every night, click EOD Clear Console. It downloads a full Excel CSV sheet of the day's sales and clears the daily Firebase & local counter console so the next morning starts fresh.
