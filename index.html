How Cell Planet Khammam ERP & POS Works

1. Global Rapid Barcode & IMEI Scanner (Stock-In + POS Billing): Scan any barcode or IMEI with your USB/Bluetooth scanner anywhere on the page! In the POS Tab, it immediately opens the device specs & IMEI verification (or adds in 0.1s if 1-Beep Instant Add is checked). In the Stock-In Tab, scanning an existing barcode instantly adds +1 to stock, or pops up the registration modal for new stock.
2. Printable Barcode & Price Sticker Studio (Old Phones & Accessories): Click 🏷️ Barcode on any item in the Inventory table (or when buying back an Old Phone) to generate laser-scannable CODE128 barcode labels with Store Name, Model, Variant, IMEI, and Price. Supports 50×25mm thermal rolls, 2-up rolls, and A4 multi-sticker sheets!
